# 🧬 Lecture 07 – Sequence Alignment and Comparative Analysis

This lecture introduced participants to the fundamental concepts of **Sequence Alignment**, a core technique in Bioinformatics used to compare DNA, RNA, and protein sequences. Participants learned how sequence alignment helps identify evolutionary relationships, conserved regions, and functional similarities among biological sequences.

## 📚 Topics Covered

- Introduction to Sequence Alignment
- Identity, Similarity, and Homology
- Global vs. Local Sequence Alignment
- Pairwise Sequence Alignment
- Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA)
- Conserved Regions and Sequence Motifs
- Functional Domains in Proteins
- Introduction to Clustal Omega and ClustalW
- Biological Applications of Sequence Alignment
- Interpreting Sequence Alignment Results

By the end of this lecture, participants gained a clear understanding of sequence alignment techniques, learned how to compare biological sequences, identify conserved regions, and appreciate the importance of alignment in functional annotation, evolutionary analysis, and comparative genomics.
