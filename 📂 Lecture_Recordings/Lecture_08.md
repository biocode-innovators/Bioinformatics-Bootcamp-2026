# 🧬 Lecture 08 – Phylogenetic Analysis and Evolutionary Relationships

This lecture introduced participants to the principles of **Phylogenetic Analysis**, a fundamental approach in Bioinformatics used to study evolutionary relationships among genes, proteins, and organisms. Participants learned how phylogenetic trees are constructed, interpreted, and applied to understand common ancestry, sequence divergence, and evolutionary history.

## 📚 Topics Covered

- Introduction to Phylogenetic Analysis
- Evolutionary Relationships and Common Ancestry
- Phylogenetic Tree Terminology
- Types of Phylogenetic Trees
- Tree Construction Methods
- Distance Matrix and Sequence-Based Analysis
- Building Phylogenetic Trees using MEGA
- Tree Visualization and Interpretation
- Introduction to iTOL for Tree Annotation
- Applications of Phylogenetic Analysis in Bioinformatics

By the end of this lecture, participants gained a practical understanding of phylogenetic analysis, learned how to construct and interpret evolutionary trees, and explored how phylogenetics is applied in comparative genomics, evolutionary biology, biodiversity studies, and molecular research.
